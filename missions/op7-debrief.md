**Duration:** months 13–14 · **Rank on completion:** INCIDENT COMMANDER (of your own project)

## Situation

A project nobody can see does not help your career or anyone else. This operation packages the work
so a recruiter, an engineer and a rescue organisation can each understand it in their own time.

## Mission

Release v1.0.0, record a 3-minute demo, write a workshop-style paper draft (target: IEEE SSRR),
give a talk, and write the final after-action review.

## Build

```text

  STEP 4  Paper          -> 4-6 pages: problem, system, evaluation, limits, ethics
  STEP 5  Talk           -> a local meetup, a university group, or a THW local chapter
  STEP 6  Portfolio      -> README hero image, resume bullets with real numbers
```

## Defend it

1. Explain LANTERN in 30 seconds to a recruiter, then in 3 minutes to an engineer.
2. What is the single biggest limit of the system, and what would fix it?
3. Which result are you proudest of, and how was it measured?
