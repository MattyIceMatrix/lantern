# Glossary (EN / DE)

```text

   |  A  B  C  D  E  F  G  H  I  |     the words rescue people use,
   |  J  K  L  M  N  O  P  Q  R  |     so your code uses them too
   |  S  T  U  V  W  X  Y  Z     |
   |_____________________________|
```

| Term | German | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICS | (Führungssystem) | Incident Command System: the US standard for organising an emergency response |
| NIMS | – | National Incident Management System: the US framework ICS sits in |
| Incident Commander (IC) | Einsatzleiter | The person in overall charge of an incident |
| Operations Section | Einsatzabschnitt | The part of ICS that carries out tactical work |
| SITREP | Lagemeldung | Situation report: short, structured update on what is happening |
| Sector | Abschnitt / Suchsektor | An area assigned to one team for search |
| THW | Technisches Hilfswerk | Germany's federal civil protection agency (volunteer-based) |
| BBK | Bundesamt für Bevölkerungsschutz und Katastrophenhilfe | German Federal Office of Civil Protection and Disaster Assistance |
| DLRG | Deutsche Lebens-Rettungs-Gesellschaft | German water rescue society |
| NFPA 1006 | – | US standard for technical rescue personnel; levels Awareness, Operations, Technician |
| INSARAG | – | International Search and Rescue Advisory Group (UN); defines the international structure marking system |
| HQ10 / HQ100 / HQextrem | Hochwasser 10 / 100 / extrem | Flood scenarios: likely every ~10 years, every ~100 years, and extreme |
| DGM1 | Digitales Geländemodell 1 m | 1-metre digital terrain model |
| EMSR517 | – | Copernicus Emergency Management Service activation for the July 2021 floods |
| OSM | – | OpenStreetMap, the open map database |
| SLAM | – | Simultaneous Localisation and Mapping |
| TRL | Technologiereifegrad | Technology Readiness Level, 1 (idea) to 9 (proven in operation) |
| FMEA | FMEA | Failure Mode and Effects Analysis |
| DPIA | DSFA (Datenschutz-Folgenabschätzung) | GDPR data protection impact assessment |
| ConOps | Einsatzkonzept | Concept of Operations |
| LoRA | – | Low-Rank Adaptation, a cheap way to fine-tune an LLM |
| GRPO | – | Group Relative Policy Optimization, a reinforcement learning method for LLMs |
| RAG | – | Retrieval-Augmented Generation: the model looks up documents before answering |
| E-stop | Not-Aus | Emergency stop button |
