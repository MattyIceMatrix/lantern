## Our commitment

Everyone is welcome here regardless of background, experience, nationality, identity or ability.
We treat each other the way a good rescue team does: direct, respectful, and focused on the mission.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Remember the subject: real floods killed real people. Keep that in mind in examples and humour.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing anyone's private information.
- Using disaster victims' images or stories for shock value.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (profile contact or a private security report
marked "conduct"). Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
